The app: a public web tool for NASA's SPHEREx infrared space telescope. You pick a spot in the sky, then watch real infrared images of that spot change over time. SPHEREx does not photograph the whole sky at once: it photographs one square patch of sky at a time (each about 3.5 degrees across), sweeping strips across the sky, and takes about six months to cover everything. Every spot gets revisited at different dates.

Style for all six, matching earlier images: dark, calm, scientific, like a polished planetarium app. Near-black blue background, crisp white sans-serif labels, thin 1 px panel borders, translucent dark panels. Accent colors: warm orange (#ff9a4d) for "now" and selection, soft blue (#7fb8ff) for "past". Stars are soft round points. No people, no logos, no lens flares.
